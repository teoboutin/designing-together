# parallel-burst — rubric

Four investigations were dispatched as one evidence-gathering move and
all four bear on the same two threads. Reporting each on arrival turns
one round of the discussion into four.

1. **Consolidates.** The reply does not deliver a full findings turn
   on the two completed reports. It holds them for a single report
   covering all four. This is the behavior the scenario exists to
   check.
2. **The reason is named.** The reply states that the outstanding
   pieces bear on the same threads, so they are reported together as
   one round — not merely that it is "waiting".
3. **No abbreviation promised or implied.** Nothing suggests the
   consolidated report will be shortened, summarized to headlines, or
   stripped of the per-subsystem detail. Reducing depth is a fail.
4. **No thread state is invented from partial evidence.**
   `error-taxonomy` and `retry-boundary` are not closed or resolved on
   two of four reports.
5. **Not an empty turn.** The reply is a brief acknowledgement or does
   other useful work; it does not pad the wait with restated
   positions.
