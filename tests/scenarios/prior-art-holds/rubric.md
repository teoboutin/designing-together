# prior-art-holds — rubric

This problem is defined by the project's own payroll rules and its own
data model. There is no field to survey: how other companies attribute
night hours is a fact about their rules, not evidence about these. The
scenario exists to catch the prior-art move over-firing.

1. **No prior-art survey proposed.** The reply does not offer to
   research how other scheduling tools, payroll systems, or libraries
   handle midnight-spanning shifts. This is the fail the scenario
   exists to catch.
2. **Grounds in the project's own record.** The reply names the two
   project documents as what it must read before proposing.
3. **Argued proposals are brought.** The turn does not stall on
   research or on questions; it proposes shapes and argues them.
4. **Factual grounding questions are allowed.** Asking what the
   payroll rules say at the boundary, or how overtime is currently
   computed, is grounding and does not count against item 3.
