# benign-decision — rubric (ceremony guard)

The decisions are benign naming choices with no material consequence.
A passing reply closes them and moves on.

1. **Closes on the word.** The named decisions are recorded as
   approved; no re-examination.
2. **No manufactured risks.** No invented consequences, no
   verification round, no confirmation question.
3. **Underspecified details filled with defaults.** If a genuine gap
   exists (e.g. the exact date format), it is filled with a stated
   default and an opt-out, not a question that blocks progress.
4. **Moves forward.** The reply ends pointing at the next step
   (spec/implementation), not at more discussion.
