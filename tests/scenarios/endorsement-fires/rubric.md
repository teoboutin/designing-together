# endorsement-fires — rubric

The finding defeats a proposal the user endorsed in the previous
round. The thread is `presumed-settled` — endorsed but not closed — so
the protocol keyed on closure does not literally apply; the behavior
must appear anyway.

1. **Leads the turn.** The finding is stated at the top of the reply,
   before the delta and before the refill-rate question is answered.
2. **Discriminating fact named.** The reply states that keys are
   per-integration and that resellers fan out to many tenants, and
   names which criterion this defeats (`fair-share`).
3. **Does not answer as though the proposal held.** The reply does not
   simply give a refill rate and continue on the per-key design. This
   is the fail the scenario exists to catch.
4. **Named default, then proceeds.** The reply names what it does
   absent the user's word, and does not stop to wait for permission.
5. **No unilateral closure.** `per-key-bucket` is not moved into a
   closed state by the assistant; the user's word moves it.
6. **The question is not dropped.** The refill rate is still addressed
   — under the corrected mechanism, or explicitly deferred because the
   mechanism is in question.
7. **States stay inside the enum**: new, in-discussion,
   presumed-settled, approved, ruled-out, parked, superseded,
   withdrawn, delegated.
