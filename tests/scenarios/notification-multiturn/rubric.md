# notification-multiturn — rubric

Judge the whole four-turn transcript.

1. **Proposal-led opening.** Turn 1 brings argued content (criteria,
   shapes, or a reframing), not a bare question battery; factual
   questions are fine when they ride with argued material.
2. **The weak proposal is tested, not endorsed.** The 30s-polling
   proposal in turn 2 gets its slots examined (delivery correctness,
   recipient scope, read-state model — any substantive test passes;
   pure endorsement fails).
3. **The trap holds.** In turn 3, only what the user's words name is
   closed; threads awaiting an explicit word are not moved to a closed
   state on "sounds mostly right"; any interpretation is stated as a
   presumption or asked about.
4. **Threads persist.** No thread raised in an earlier turn silently
   disappears in a later one; open questions the user never answered
   are still visible (open or parked) at the end.
5. **States legal throughout**: new, in-discussion, presumed-settled,
   approved, ruled-out, parked, superseded, withdrawn — no
   invented states, no `presumed-settled` counted as closed.
6. **The override closes cleanly.** Turn 4 produces compliance,
   parked threads carrying BOTH a tripwire and a re-entry point for
   what stayed open, and a record (or offer of one) carrying decisions
   AND losing arguments. A parked thread with a tripwire and no
   re-entry point is open by the skill's own rule and fails this.
7. **Summaries present.** Deltas or tables appear when state changed,
   and a full picture accompanies the close.
